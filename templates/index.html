Scope and limitations

This retrospective prototype uses quarterly public data for New York facility counties. The panel covers {{ panel_start }} through {{ panel_end }}; therefore, the one-quarter-ahead option is the first quarter after the latest panel period, not a live current-quarter forecast. Facility county is not necessarily the patient's county of residence. Publicly suppressed facility values may understate totals in affected county-quarters. A live operational version would require a current internal encounter feed and refreshed predictors.
